# 📦 Work-In-Progress (WIP) Storage

Members may store **one** stained glass project in progress on-site.

### 📋 Requirements
* **Form Required:** You must complete the *Stained Glass WIP Form* and place it on your board.
* **Identification:** Boards without a form will be treated as unclaimed.
* **What to Store:** Only glass pieces, patterns, and templates. **Tools and supplies may not be stored on project boards.**

### ⏳ Duration & Unclaimed Projects
* **Active Status:** If space is limited, you may be contacted for an update. Projects untouched for several months may be cleared.
* **Reclamation:** The Makerspace may reclaim materials if no form is present, membership is inactive, or attempts to contact the owner fail.
