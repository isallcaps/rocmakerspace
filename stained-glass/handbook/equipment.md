# 🛠️ Equipment Use & Grinder SOP

## General Tool Care
* **Responsibility:** Use tools responsibly and return them to their designated locations.
* **Integrity:** Do not modify or disassemble tools.
* **Cleaning:** Clean tools after use to remove glass dust, solder, flux, or residue.
* **Reporting:** Notify the [Area Manager](./index.md) immediately of damaged or malfunctioning tools.

---

## 💎 Glass Grinder Standard Operating Procedure
**By using the grinder, you agree to leave it ready for the next person.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Glass powder is hazardous to breathe.** Never operate the grinder without water. Ensure at least ¼ inch of water is in the reservoir and the sponge/bit are thoroughly wet before starting.

### After You Finish (Required Cleaning)
1. **Disassemble:** Remove the grinding wheel with a hex key and lift the plastic grid.
2. **Water Disposal:** Pour excess water into a **bucket**, NOT the sink.
3. **Sludge Removal:** Wipe glass sludge with a wet paper towel; bag it before discarding.
4. **Rinse:** Rinse the grid and reservoir carefully—do not let glass powder go down the drain.
5. **Final Wipe:** Wipe all surrounding surfaces. There should be **no white haze** left behind.
