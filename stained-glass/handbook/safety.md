# ⚠️ Safety Guidelines

> [!WARNING]
> **Ventilation:** Turn on the ventilation fan when soldering to reduce exposure to fumes.

* **PPE:** Safety glasses are required when cutting or grinding. Masks or respirators are highly recommended.
* **Glass Dust:** Always **wet-wipe** dust. Never dry sweep glass powder.
* **Chemicals:** Use gloves for patina and keep drinks closed and away from soldering stations.
* **Attire:** Tie back long hair and avoid loose clothing.
