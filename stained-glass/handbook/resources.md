# 📦 Resources & Supplies

This section outlines the shared resources provided by the Rochester Makerspace and the materials members are expected to provide for their own projects.

## 1. What Makerspace Provides

### 1.1 Project Boards & Glass
* **Project Boards:** Homasote project boards are available for active work.
* **Member Glass Bins:** You may use glass **only** from the labeled clear plastic bins. These contain a mix of opaque and transparent scrap glass.

> ### ⚠️ Class Glass Policy
> Full or partial glass sheets located **outside** the clear bins are reserved strictly for classes. Extra glass is cycled into the member bins periodically.

### 1.2 Tools & Consumables
All Makerspace-provided tools and materials must remain on-site at all times.

| Category | Items Provided |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Power Tools** | Glass grinders, Soldering irons |
| **Hand Tools** | Glass cutters, Running pliers, Breaking pliers, Small brushes, Scissors, Sharpies |
| **Consumables** | Flux, Black patina, Copper patina, Wax |

---

## 2. What Members Must Provide
Members are responsible for supplying their own primary materials and personal safety gear:

* **Project Materials:** Glass sheets (for projects beyond scrap size), Solder (60/40), Copper foil (7/32" black-backed recommended), and Jump rings.
* **Personal Tools:** X-Acto knives, Sharpies, or any specialized hand tools.
* **Safety Gear:** Safety glasses and masks.

---

## 3. Donations
We gratefully accept glass sheets, offcuts, and tools in good working condition.

* **Small Donations:** May be placed in the designated donation area.
* **Large Donations:** Please coordinate with the **Office Manager**'
